Who We Are

Engineers' Council

The Engineers’ Council (E-Council) is the student council for the College of Engineering.

As the umbrella organization for undergraduate engineering groups, we plan and advertise college events, distribute funding to organizations, and advocate for student interests.

Our Organization

Who Are E-Council's Members?

Executive Leadership

An executive leadership team comprised of interdisciplinary engineering students.

Committee Directors

Committee director(s) who lead the various council committees.

General Members

General engineering student members working within a specific committee.
