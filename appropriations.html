The Process

How Appropriations Works

To be eligible for funding, an appropriations form must be filled out. In addition, percentage funding is influenced by fulfilling specific requirements set by E-Council.

Each semester, as the student council for the College of Engineering, the Engineers’ Council oversees the process of distributing funding provided by the college to engineering clubs and organizations on campus.

The fund being distributed is made up of a small portion of each student in the College of Engineering’s Engineering Enhancement Fee. As such, there are specific guidelines for how these funds can be used.

The process of distributing funding to engineering clubs and organizations is overseen by the current E-Council Internal & External Treasurers. The Treasurers contact engineering clubs and organizations with schedules, due dates, and documents explaining how the process is run.

Appropriations decisions are ultimately made by the Department Representatives under the advisement of the E-Council Treasurers.

The Treasurers ensure that funding requests are allowable under guidelines approved by Engineering Academic Affairs, align with E-Council priorities, and that each club or organization has a fair opportunity to compete for funding.

DR Rubric

A non-binding rubric provided to Department Representatives to help evaluate club and organization presentations.

The DR Rubric is intended to help Department Representatives evaluate presentations based on the guidelines and requirements outlined in the appropriations documents. In general, a higher point score correlates with more funding, although the rubric is not completely comprehensive and this may not always be the case.
